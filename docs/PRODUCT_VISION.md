# Product vision

NEXUS connects people, knowledge, academic activity, and institutional work. Obsidian Labs proposes SIT Mysuru as the first pilot. The release is intended for engineering education, with applicable VTU schemes subject to institutional verification.

The five layers are identity (who can act), knowledge (what the institution knows), activity (what is happening), workflow (what needs to happen), and intelligence (what can be understood and assisted). Product domains include academics and learning, student growth, research and innovation, campus activities, administration, resources and operations, careers and alumni, and institutional governance.

AURA will help people discover, understand, and act on authorized information. NEXUS should track whether these actions led to useful educational or operational outcomes. Track grading turnaround, service resolution time, and successful engagement against measured baselines. Track access violations, accessibility failures, and accuracy alongside them.
