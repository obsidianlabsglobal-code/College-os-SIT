# Parent transport view (design only, disabled)

No parent route, permission, enrollment or tracking is shipped for the pilot. A future institutional setting must default to off. It must not be a URL flag, a public share code, or implicit access by registering as a guardian.

If SIT approves a later version, use the verified guardian relationship workflow and the student's explicit, specific and revocable consent for a bus trip view. Re-check that relationship and permission on every read. Share only the assigned bus's transport status, its approved route and trip window. Do not show the student's pickup choice, boarding confirmation, personal location, backup location contribution, attendance, identity of other riders, or the driver's details. End access immediately at trip end, withdrawal, or relationship revocation. Include audit evidence and tests for expired, revoked and cross-institution relationships. If consent and institutional policy are unresolved, remain disabled.
