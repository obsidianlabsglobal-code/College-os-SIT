# Transport 5b: disruption notice implementation handoff
Status: deterministic copy and decision helpers locally tested. No app wiring, migration, PR, delivery, institutional permission, or working production integration.

## States and ownership
- Start from the authoritative trip and current route revision, not a browser-declared vehicle. For driver actions, resolve the bound boarding device and active trip, lock that trip and recheck fresh bus-owned stationary evidence; no rider backup or stale speed qualifies. If not provable, disable driver incident reporting with a clear reason and keep the office path available. Office uses an active transport grant.
- Create one tenant/trip-scoped incident linked to the existing transport_incident case kind. The initial post, each office-edited bilingual update, and closure are distinct immutable notice revisions. Keep attendance notes and transport logs separate. Do not show raw coordinates, driver data, injured identities, or student rosters in messages. Display the nearest verified named stop or defer pickup instructions to authorized office operators if location is unclear.
- Snapshot intended recipients and template field values at publication. Classify as onboard from an accepted boarding record or waiting from the trip's frozen route/stop assignment and progress. Do not recalculate mid-trip preferences. Enqueue a text-only lead language beside immutable delivery receipts; source each rider's verified English/Kannada preference. Future messages use saved/current approved recipient preference and preserve prior send/read evidence. Review both localized variants with a Kannada speaker and transport office before pilot use.
- The current promise expires at its persisted timestamp. An internal worker posts one office warning per incident/deadline if no new approved update or closure is recorded. Repeated updates replace the current open deadline in the incident but keep promise history. Distinguish queued, delivered where verified, and read through a signed-in receipt.
- Resolve only through a scoped operator with actual arrival evidence for the trip/replacement; if cancellation remains final and no bus arrived, use a separate final cancellation status, never invent an arrival. The all-clear fanout strictly uses the union of recipients notified on that same tenant/trip/incident, deduped.
- Accident safety check is for confirmed stranded recipients, not everyone reading a route notice. In the app, distinguish saved response, pending network, and confirmed staff acknowledgment. A need-help response goes to authorized transport dispatch without identity exposure to other riders, with fallback approved by SIT; do not present the feature as a substitute for emergency services.
- Separate notification authority from attendance authority. Class teacher and mentor receive only the minimum scoped reason/time for confirmed impacted riders, not incident or injury details. First-period bus-delay is an annotation/effective excuse to an otherwise late mark for the exact trip service date and student, not a conversion of absence to presence. Teacher decisions win; preserve original observation and audit trail. Do not infer attendance from route membership or no-show status. Use boarding, or a separately verified rider/stop impact record when boarding cannot be established. Integration owns the attendance adapter.
- Office incident card lists chronological revisions, delivery/read receipts restricted to authorized staff, actual resolution time, and the replacement linkage. Aggregate historical incidents by route, segment/named stop and time band; don't infer a person's behavior or attach private identities to insights.

## Acceptance beyond pure tests
1. Driver valid bound device, fresh stopped bus: create issue. Moving, inactive trip, stale location, mismatched or revoked device and unauthorized actor must not write or send. Office can raise in the same stopped-evidence failure without impersonating driver.
2. Recipient language correct in both inbox and permitted offline/push paths. English edits cannot overwrite Kannada body. Each language has waiting and aboard variants, and a distinct instruction; no map coordinates or unverified emergency information. The need-help action is audience-limited and acknowledged only after persistence.
3. Promise overdue with no approved update: office sees exactly one warning. Publishing an update closes the old deadline and sets a new one; late posts do not erase the prior warning. Repeat worker safely.
4. All-clear uses actual (not planned) arrival and reaches every previous authorized incident recipient once, including a rider who moved from waiting to onboard. Unrelated tenant and trip content never appears. Replacement ETA remains restricted to affected eligible riders.
5. Delay annotation affects only verified impacted riders' first period on the exact service day; manual decisions and absence unaffected. Teacher gets one event, can override, and changing sections later does not expand access. Unassigned mentor or teacher gets nothing.
6. Browser: 390 px, ordinary stopped-driver setup, bilingual reader, resolution card, stale/no-signal and empty states. Fresh migrations/seed and complete repo QA still required after integration.

## Source checks for the base transport work
- [Traccar OsmAnd protocol](https://www.traccar.org/osmand/) is for modern Traccar Client and lists query/POST fields (device ID, location and configurable speed; default knots). Device ID is not assumed to be authentication. Confirm the exact installed client build, request encoding and support for credential conveyance with local device captures before claiming compatibility; [legacy Client protocol](https://www.traccar.org/traccar-client-protocol/) is different. Capture only synthetic traces; never retain cellular/Wi-Fi metadata or private device identifiers in app logs. Account for the client's local retry/buffer behavior and parked-state freshness separately.
- [OSMF raster tile policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/): use optional, lazy HTTPS tiles on visible map only, with attribution, caching and valid referer. No offline downloads/prefetch; do not send private stop labels or telemetry in tile URLs. Audit the app's global referrer policy before enabling the map; any override in shared headers belongs to integration. Text route must stay usable without maps.
- Rider backup location must remain off until separate consent/college approval. Update the earlier bus-only privacy draft before distribution if that optional feature enters the approved pilot; track no rider location for boarding/SOS. Keep real data, any notification delivery, and live hardware out of this demo.
