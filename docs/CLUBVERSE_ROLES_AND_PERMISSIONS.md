# CLUBVERSE roles and permissions

NEXUS student and faculty workspaces can propose communities matching their audience. They can discover active eligible clubs within their institution. An administrator can review submitted proposals and event approval requests, suspend/archive clubs and approve a leadership transfer. A reviewer cannot approve their own creation. An institutional administrator does not receive the private member directory or attendance evidence through the review view.

Active club members can see member-only events. Membership and leadership do not grant institutional roles. Leads and officers manage members and events in the granted club only. Leads manage advisor/officer grants and request transfer to an active member. Faculty advisorship records an association and carries no officer action privileges. Ending membership ends its delegated grants. The lead transfers authority through the separately approved handover.

The current recognition policy uses the institution-admin role. A configurable institutional rule editor, cofounders, scheduled officer terms and custom alumni eligibility are planned.
