# Roadmap

## Release 1

Target HTTPS acceptance on December 1, 2026 IST. Deliver identity and roles, academic administration, assignments and published feedback, notices, enrollment-letter requests, leadership reporting, CI and deployment verification. Target an earlier candidate deployment for corrections and recovery rehearsal.

## Release 1.1

Build attendance, timetables, academic resources, events, opportunities, projects and additional student service workflows after Release 1 is stable. Add a guardian workspace after approval of access and consent policy.

## Future

Expand research, operations, career development, alumni and institutional governance. Evaluate AURA grounded search and assistance, the Institutional Outcome Graph, learning recovery, opportunity equity, resource simulation and governed agents. Decisions on institutional integrations, imports and adoption depend on pilot evidence and authorized access.
