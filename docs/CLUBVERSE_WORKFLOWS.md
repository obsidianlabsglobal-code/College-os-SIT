# CLUBVERSE workflows

Recognition uses draft → submitted → under review → approved, rejected or changes requested. A founder revises changes requested and resubmits. Approval atomically creates the active club and its founder membership and lead grant. Administrator suspension prevents actions, and archival is terminal.

Open joining activates an eligible person directly. Officer approval starts with a request. Invitation-only policy requires an officer invitation and member acceptance. The club lead cannot end their own membership through ordinary removal. An active incoming member receives the new lead grant and authorized handover notes when a distinct institution reviewer approves the transfer. Outgoing grants end in the same transaction. The effective date is approval time.

Officers draft events, submit for administrator approval, and publish/open registration after approval. Registration closes manually or becomes unavailable when the event start passes. Officers record actual participation separately from registrations and can complete an event. Completion shows totals separately. No waitlist, cancellation by participant, automatic approval, files or automatic participation claims exist in this slice.
