# Campus Change Brief: first useful NEXUS 2040 slice

One published future class, same time, instructor and section, different approved room. Admin sees up to three non-conflicting choices using recorded capacity, active student count and institution-seeded directional room compatibility. Review shows the old and proposed room and lets the operator explicitly confirm. The server rechecks the session revision, actor, instructor, candidate capacity and room conflicts under the schedule and destination-booking locks. The room change and append-only receipt commit together. Student Today and Timetable use the same scoped receipt. They do not claim email, push delivery, or student acknowledgment. A newer revision supersedes the visible brief; older changes remain in review evidence.

Do not treat similar room names or matching seat count as equivalent lab equipment. SIT has to approve room pairs, current safety/accessibility readiness, operator access, roster and official fallback. No general room-equivalence management UI, external notification, automated physical device control, route solver, or real SIT records are included. Capacity is checked against the current active roster at review and publish; later roster changes need institutional monitoring.

## Research adoption

- Campus Change Brief: first production-shaped rehearsal, built on existing timetable and booking authority.
- Source and scope-aware next action: add a current published receipt to Student Today, never expose another schedule.
- Human confirmation and replay protection: apply to a bounded change, rather than a generic AI executor.
- Next useful increments: revision-scoped student acknowledgment with an accessible official fallback; correction inbox; faculty feedback handoff reuse; Lab Passport prelab evidence; dependable pilot sign-in and restoration.
- Defer sensors, solver-driven multi-section repairs, model-based grading, fee posting, private AI, guardian access, and engineering complexity until the evidence and authority are in place.
