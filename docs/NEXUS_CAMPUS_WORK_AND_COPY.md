## Where students and leaders are represented

- A **student passport** is the student's own profile and official academic record. Distinguish enrollment and course roster from advising and sensitive cases. The current **Attendance workbench** shows a searchable, assigned class roster and the present/absent mark, not an all-college directory. Next design: a separate registrar-scoped searchable enrollment directory; show only the fields necessary for each job, with HOD department summaries and faculty assigned sections. SIS imports require owner approval, reconciliation and effective dates. No cross-college roster export by default.
- An academic **class representative** is scoped to that section's notices and coordination, never grades or attendance by default. A **club member** belongs to a club. A **role** is a specific grant (President, Secretary/Joint Secretary, Treasurer, Tech, Publicity, Documentation, Logistics, event volunteer, etc.); customize labels without widening grants. A faculty adviser is a separate designation; event approval requires an actual grant.
- A **dated appointment** links a member to a specific club and scope from start to expiry. A **one-event assignment** grants only event tasks, not access to private membership or academic records. Cross-department events explicitly opt in named co-hosts and routes for venue review and independent publication. Event RSVP is not verified participation. Handover revokes outgoing privileges and records responsibility transfer. No self-appointment or copying an academic entitlement from an office title.
- Existing CLUBVERSE supports fictional recognition, scoped membership, proposals, event approvals, registrations, participation evidence and leadership transfer on the tested branch. Configurable titles, dated appointment rules and department co-hosting workflow are proposed. Recommended proof: one fictional EEE–ECE co-hosted workshop, correct reviewers, no cross-club roster access, and expired officer denied.

## Reusable one-click copy

| From a source of truth | Draft contents | Audience and gate |
|---|---|---|
| Saved attendance | Class/section, date/time, faculty, counts | Assigned faculty sees a copy-ready official-group update; full student roll stays in NEXUS. Named absence example is fictional only pending real authorization. |
| Published room change | Class, time, old room, destination | Authorized scheduler can copy for affected class/staff. Only a current published revision is offered on page load; refresh if a change is still being coordinated. No reason text or roster in the copied message. |
| Published upcoming club event | Organizer, time, listed location, registration and audience | Current officer can copy only for the event's approved audience. No RSVP list or requirements copied. |
| Personal leave or permission request | Recipient, dates, optional reason when appropriate, requester's authenticated name and role | User reviews and copies. Not submitted, approved, saved to a record, or sent. |
| Next proposals | Published notices, approved exam timetable updates, service-status replies, roster-safe lab or transport disruptions | Draft from verified state. Repeat the audience gate and omit private payloads. Finance, sensitive cases and bulk named reports require a channel policy and explicit approval. |

An official issue workflow is different from copying a personal request. For bonafide or enrollment certificates, recommendation, duty letters, permissions for field trips and formal leave records, first confirm who may request and issue each template, required evidence, exact sign-off, correction/withdrawal behavior and verification method. Do not use a template to make an unofficial draft look signed. For an operational work record, capture request ID, team and allowed audience, start or due date, owner and status, minimum evidence, confirmation, and version / last change. Reuse those records for dashboards; do not make work teams retype numbers to feed them.
