# Implementation dependencies and release gates

Use the [master registry](NEXUS_MASTER_FEATURE_REGISTRY.md) for scope and status. Prefixes below refer to its stable IDs. One Next.js application and PostgreSQL own the five layers: institutional identity, permissioned knowledge, dated activity, transactional workflows and governed intelligence. Resolve identity first on every request. Domain services own authoritative transitions. AURA consumes those same services. Share scheduling and workflow machinery when a second real consumer demonstrates the same semantics.

## Coordination decisions

Academic course workspaces are the next independent Wave 2 implementation because current catalog and roster already supply the authority. Venue booking remains the first proposed CLUBVERSE extension; see [slice designs](CLUBVERSE_NEXT_SLICES.md). Each can be reviewed separately from the hosted foundation. Faculty mission control and evaluation queues (FC-001, FC-011) are tested on feature/faculty-command-center and await review. Their counts cover every offering with an active faculty roster, so an institution-approved definition of the current academic term should precede wider faculty use; structured rubrics (FC-010) and the feedback-drafting assistant (AGFT-004) should follow faculty feedback on the queue. A future wave can have a previously tested prerequisite on a feature branch. This does not move that wave onto the release branch.

Attendance corrections (SA-021) are decided only by the class's current teacher (the session's faculty with an active roster). The student is the only one who can request one. Acceptance writes a new attendance revision in the same transaction, so later reports (DH-009, LE-002, SA-006) read it without a separate path. HOD or office override of a teacher's decision is not implemented and needs an approved policy first. Calendar (SA-010), catalog (SA-002) and CSV exports (SA-022, FC-028) are read-only projections of existing records and add no new authority.

Course plans (DH-022 slice) are approved or returned only by an HOD of the course's department. Approval freezes the topics, and delivery logs can link only to approved topics. Reopening an approved plan is not implemented. Course materials are written by the offering's current teachers and read by its roster and department HOD. Mentors are assigned by an institution administrator; a replacement ends the previous pairing and its mentor loses access, while the records are kept. The executive report is principal-only.

Cases (AD-020 slice) are one engine with per-kind authority. Support cases are opened by an HOD or teacher for a student on their rosters, assigned by the HOD to a teacher in that department, and hidden from the student. Grievances are opened by a student, triaged and owned by the administrative office, and shown to the principal only after 7 days open, without notes. Only the owner records an outcome. Other office workflows (enrollment letters, fees) have not been migrated onto the engine. The weekly digest runs inside the worker loop and needs no audit event.

Examination sittings are drafted, published and cancelled only by an institution administrator acting as the exam cell. Publication is refused if a published exam already uses the section, room or invigilator at that time, if the room or invigilator has a class, or if a confirmed booking holds the room. Improvement actions: the principal assigns institution actions to an HOD, admin or the principal; an HOD assigns department actions to a department teacher or themself. Only the owner records progress or closes an action.

Teaching allocation (DH-007) splits authority. The HOD proposes a teacher only for offerings in their department, and only an institution administrator approves. Approval writes the faculty roster row in the same transaction. Offboarding (ID-009) is admin-only, refuses the caller's own account and other admins, and ends posts, rosters and mentoring pairings together; cases and requests the person owns are reported for reassignment, not moved. Project teams are formed and reviewed only by the offering's current teachers.

Policies (AD-021) are drafted only by an institution administrator and take effect only after the principal approves the version with a reason. A rejected version never takes effect. Attendance eligibility (SA-007) reads the latest approved attendance policy version and is an explanation, not an official eligibility decision. New case kinds:
- Facility repairs: office-owned; the reporter can reopen within 14 days.
- Integrity appeals: student-only, tied to the published integrity policy version and their own published assignment, and reviewed in the course's department by its HOD or a teacher the HOD assigns.
- Department requests: student-only, routed by the HOD.

Development records and research projects are decided by an HOD of the department the teacher teaches in. An HOD cannot verify their own records. The HOD also chooses whether an approved project is disclosed to the institution, and only disclosed projects reach the principal. Catalog name corrections are admin-only and keep the old name, reason and actor.

My work (AD-018) only collects items. Each item comes from its own workflow's read model and authority rule, so the queue never grants access. Institution calendar dates and catalog visibility are admin-only. A hidden offering stays visible to its roster.

Delegation (AD-019): an admin lends exactly one duty (exam cell or institution calendar) to a current faculty member or HOD, with an expiry of at most 365 days. The admin can revoke it. While it is live, the delegate passes that duty's checks in their own role; nothing else widens. Certificates are drafted and issued only by admins. Public verification shows only the type, institution, date, status and holder initials.

Meetings are visible only to their participants. Only the organizer records minutes and assigns actions, and only to participants; each owner closes their own action. Representation issues are readable by every current student, who can add support once, and the office owns the response. Portfolio entries are restricted to the student's own verified records, and who can read them follows the profile visibility. Decision scenarios are principal-only and read-only.

Source vocabulary has changed over time. The original eight product domains describe subject areas. Later OS names describe user workspaces that cross those areas. Retain both views, with shared identity and records. A prototype name confers no evidence of a working implementation. Operational GPS, model suggestions and policy-sensitive disclosure retain explicit gates.

## Change procedure

Assign a new permanent ID or revise an existing row, preserve its history in Git, record founder source, users, authority, data, dependencies, uncertainty, acceptance journey and wave. Capture conflict and proposed resolution before a material reprioritization. Update tests and evidence with a revision when status changes. Record hosted, user-validation and production acceptance separately. Explicit deprecation retains its ID, reason, successor and owner decision.

The booking implementation adds the first concrete event-to-resource dependency. Follow [resource booking](RESOURCE_BOOKING.md) for the baseline and boundary checks. Compare attendance and timetable source readiness with institutional staff before picking the next academic slice. Attendance needs verified session and correction policy. Timetable needs an authorized schedule and room-conflict rules. Scope interviews and workflow observation to authorized institutional participants and record the baseline for the IDT project.

EEE pilot onboarding (AD-002, ID-009): the [local admissions rehearsal](SIT_ONBOARDING_PREVIEW.md) precedes verified Google identity, account binding, role provisioning, revocation and a hosted load rehearsal. Privileged roles and real roster handling require SIT-authorized decisions.

Letters, preferences and support services (AD-020, SL-010, SL-011, SL-015): submitted letters are office-coordinated cases readable only by their sender and current admins. Preferences change only what the member sees, never another member's access. Support services are published and withdrawn by admins only. Work split with Codex: only Claude changes `case_record` kinds and `notifications()`; Codex requests them in the ledger (see `docs/handoff/NEXUS_CODEX_PARALLEL_PLAN.pdf`).
